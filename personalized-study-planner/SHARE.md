# Send this to another person

The folder is for you (or for building a GPT/Gem/Skill). A student in Instagram DMs needs three things, not thirty files.

## What to send

1. This sentence:

> Paste this into ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini. Under it, put your exam date, how many minutes you actually have, your topic list, what’s done, and what’s weak. Save the card it gives you. Tomorrow paste the card and type TODAY.

2. The prompt in [`QUICK-PROMPT.md`](./QUICK-PROMPT.md) (full [`STUDY-OS.md`](./STUDY-OS.md) if they will live in one chat).
3. Optionally [`START-HERE.md`](./START-HERE.md) if they want the loop explained.

Do not send `core/` or `native/` unless they asked how to *build* a GPT/Gem/Skill.

## What they should see in the first reply

If the first reply has no **COMMAND CENTER**, no **3 non-negotiables**, and no **`- [ ]` ticks for today**, they pasted into a chat that ignored the prompt. Tell them: new chat, paste the prompt as the first message, details underneath.

## Caption you can use (Instagram / WhatsApp)

```text
Not a 30-day fantasy timetable.

You give it:
• syllabus / chapters
• exam date
• minutes you actually have
• what’s done + what’s weak

It gives you:
• an honest verdict (on track / tight / not enough time)
• this week’s 3 non-negotiables
• a tick list for today with a score on every block
• a weak-topic drill list
• a card you paste back tomorrow so it remembers

Save the card. That’s the whole app.
```

## If you built them a GPT / Gem / Skill

Send the **link** (GPT) or say “open the Study OS Gem / Skill”. Then:

```text
First message: exam, date, minutes, topics, done, weak, minutes today.
Every day after: paste your card + TODAY or DONE.
```

Still tell them the card is the save file. Custom GPTs and Gems forget the previous chat.

## What not to promise

- It will not study for them.
- It will not fit 12 chapters into 6 hours without naming what gets dropped.
- It will not nag them at 19:00. That’s a calendar reminder, not this.
