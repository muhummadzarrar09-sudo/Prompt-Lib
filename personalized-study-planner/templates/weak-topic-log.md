## Promote rule

A topic leaves this list only when:

- confidence is ≥ 4 on the card, and
- you scored mostly correct on a recent quiz or past-paper slice covering it

If you fail a drill, streak goes back to 0 and next due is tomorrow.

## Spacing

`fail or new → 1 day → 3 days → 7 days → 14 days`

Do the drill on the due date even if you “already studied that chapter” this week. The point is retrieval, not exposure.
